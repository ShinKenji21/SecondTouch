          What am I looking at?

          SecondTouch watches a live football match, turns every action into numbers, picks out the moments that matter and explains why they matter, for three kinds of viewer and in three languages. The match is simulated and the clubs are fictional.

          The screen

          1. Header picks how you watch: viewing mode, language, your club and one metric to pin on screen.
          2. Pitch shows all 22 players live. Pass lines run green (easy) to red (hard), dribbles are dotted, shots carry an xG bubble, and set pieces, VAR and goals get a label.
          3. Replay bar: pause, step, scrub, jump to a goal, change speed, then GO LIVE.
          4. Live AI card explains the current moment. Its label says who wrote it: the instant local engine or Azure OpenAI.
          5. Mode panel changes with the viewing mode (below).
          6. Live Event Log: every action as it happens. Click one to replay it.
          7. Tabs below the pitch: full stats, tactics, key events with their stories, charts, Talk to AI, and the data and overlay feed.

          Three ways to watch

          • Analyst Numbers first: both teams side by side, the last action's pressure, pass difficulty and ball speed.
          • Casual The story at a glance: who's on top, simple bars and the goals. Pick a club and the commentary cheers for it.
          • Player Cam Follow one player: their card, estimated fitness, stats against the team and a heat map. Click any player on the pitch.

          Try this

          • Press START MATCH, then click a player on the pitch.
          • Press Space to pause, ← → to step, L to go live.
          • Switch the language to 日本語 or Español: everything is rewritten at once.
          • In Talk to AI, ask “Who looks the most tired?”

          Key terms

          xG (expected goals)
          How likely a shot was to go in, 0 to 1, from distance, angle and pressure. 0.30+ is a big chance.
          xA (expected assists)
          The xG of the shots a player set up with their pass.
          ⚡ Impact moment
          A moment the engine flags as worth explaining: goals, big chances, line-breaking passes, high-press turnovers, cards, penalties, VAR.
          Pressure index
          0-100, how tightly the player on the ball is closed down, from the nearest defender's distance.
          Pass difficulty
          0-100 from the pass length, direction, pressure and how tightly the receiver is marked.
          Momentum
          Who is on top right now, from recent final-third actions, shots, line breaks and ball wins.
          Control vs chaos
          Control: one team keeps the ball with few turnovers. Chaos: possession keeps changing hands.
          Field tilt
          Each team's share of the play in the final third: who is pinning the other back.
          PPDA
          Opponent passes allowed per defensive action. Lower means a more aggressive press.
          Est. fitness
          Our estimate, not a measurement: starts at 100 and drops with minutes played and sprints, faster for players with a low stamina rating.
          📣 Pushing forward / 🧱 Sitting deep
          What a team's shape shows late on: a higher line, harder pressing and more direct play, or a team dropping off and slowing the game.
